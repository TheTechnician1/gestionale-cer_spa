Download Videafactory

Install the Premiere Pro extension, then open the Videafactory panel and activate it with your license key.

Download installer Need help?

Download access

The installer is available from the official Videafactory release download. Your Videafactory license key is sent separately by email after purchase.

Install steps

  1. Close Adobe Premiere Pro.
  2. Run the downloaded installer.
  3. Open Premiere Pro and launch the Videafactory panel.
  4. Enter your license key in the License section.
